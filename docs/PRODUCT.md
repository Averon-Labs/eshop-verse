# Product Definition

## Purpose

EShop Verse is a production-oriented e-commerce platform that enables customers to browse, search, and purchase products through a mobile application, while providing merchants with tools to manage their catalog, orders, and customers through an admin dashboard.

---

## Target Users

### Customers
- Mobile shoppers using Android devices
- Users who want to browse products, manage wishlists, place orders, and track deliveries

### Administrators / Merchants
- Store owners and staff managing product catalog, inventory, and orders
- Business analysts reviewing sales data and customer metrics

---

## Customer Application

### Confirmed Requirements
- User registration and authentication
- Product browsing with categories
- Product search and filtering
- Product detail views with images and descriptions
- Shopping cart management
- Wishlist management
- Order placement
- Order history and tracking
- User profile management
- Product reviews and ratings
- Push notifications for order updates

### Assumptions (Require Validation)
- Single currency support initially
- Single language (English) initially
- Shipping address management
- Guest checkout may or may not be supported
- Social login providers to be determined

---

## Admin Capabilities

### Confirmed Requirements
- Product CRUD (create, read, update, delete)
- Category management
- Inventory management
- Order management and status updates
- Customer account management
- Basic analytics and reporting

### Assumptions (Require Validation)
- Role-based access control for admin users
- Bulk product operations (import/export)
- Discount and coupon management
- Dashboard with key metrics
- Admin technology stack to be decided
